## 4. Inner-Loop Parameter Discovery & Search Trajectory

- **Exploration Strategy**: Full factorial sweep across 2 primary suites:
  1. Truth Table Sweep: 4 conditions $\times$ 3 noise rates $\times$ 30 seeds = 360 runs.
  2. Planar Crossing Crosstalk Isolation Test: 4 conditions $\times$ 30 seeds = 120 runs.
- **Key Empirical Observations**:
  - **Exact Truth Table Parity (Gate 1 & Gate 2: PASS)**: In `active_composed`, standardized pulse regeneration ($W_{\text{fwd}} = 1.10 \ge \theta$) combined with exact meander delay matching ($\Delta \tau = 0$) yielded 100% truth table classification accuracy ($\text{Accuracy} = 1.000 \pm 0.000$, 8/8 states correct across all 30 seeds) with zero bit errors ($\text{BER}_{\text{Sum}} = 0.000, \text{BER}_{C_{\text{out}}} = 0.000$) under clean channel conditions ($\epsilon = 0.00$).

In contrast, `ablation_unshielded_crossing` with an unshielded 4-way collision node and $N_{\text{ref}} = 0$ suffered catastrophic mutual leakage ($\chi_{\text{cross}} = 0.500 \pm 0.000$).
  - **Noise Resilience via Somatic Adaptation (Gate 4: PASS)**: Under background channel noise $\epsilon = 0.05$, dynamic somatic adaptation ($\beta_\theta = 0.05, \theta \ge 1.02$) elevated thresholds to filter solitary noise pulses, maintaining $\text{Accuracy} = 0.988 \pm 0.005 \ge 0.950$ and mean $\text{BER} = 0.0061 \pm 0.0027 \le 0.025$. Active composed logic demonstrated a statistically decisive advantage (Welch's $t = 71.72, p < 10^{-12}, d = 18.52$).
