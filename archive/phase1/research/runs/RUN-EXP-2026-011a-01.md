## 4. Inner-Loop Parameter Discovery & Search Trajectory

- **Exploration Strategy**: Full factorial sweep across 4 conditions $\times$ 3 noise rates $\times$ 3 sequence lengths $\times$ 30 independent pseudo-random seeds ($1,080$ run cells, $54,000$ sequences).
- **Observations during Exploration**:
  - `active_associative`: Maintained 100% retrieval accuracy under clean channel ($\epsilon = 0.00$) regardless of sequence length ($L=64$, $L=256$, $L=512$). Distractor leakage remained identically zero ($\chi_{\text{distractor}} = 0.000$). At $\epsilon = 0.05$, accuracy remained high at $95.78\%$ ($\text{BER} = 0.0422$).
  - `baseline_unindexed`: Collapsed to chance accuracy on non-recency keys ($\text{Accuracy} = 0.5386$), validating the mathematical necessity of key-indexed dynamic addressing.
  - `ablation_unshielded_retrieval`: Suffered severe standing wave reflections and mutual exclusivity breakdown ($\chi_{\text{mutex}} = 0.9481$), resulting in continuous dual-rail readout collisions and chance accuracy ($50.19\%$).
  - `ablation_fixed_theta`: Matched active accuracy under clean baseline ($\epsilon = 0.00$), but degraded severely under critical noise $\epsilon = 0.05$ ($\text{Accuracy} = 0.7136, \text{BER} = 0.2864$), demonstrating the protective role of dynamic threshold adaptation.
