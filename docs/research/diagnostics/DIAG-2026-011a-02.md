## Executive Summary

Mechanical re-analysis of the already-executed `EXP-2026-011a` telemetry (WP-5c calibration workstream) identified a methodological defect in the `ablation_fixed_theta` control. No experiment was re-run for this report; all statements below are measured from preserved artifacts under [data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/).

1. **The noise-shielding ablation is confounded.** `AblationFixedTheta` is presented as a single-variable ablation of somatic threshold adaptation ($\beta_\theta$). It is not: it differs from `ActiveAssociative` in **four** parameters simultaneously ([config.rs L108-L131](src/experiments/exp_2026_011a_mva_associative_retrieval/config.rs#L108-L131)).
2. **At the published operating point the named mechanism is inert.** Sweeping $\beta_\theta$ over $[0, 0.20]$ (12 values, a $200\times$ range including zero) at the shipped hand-tuned parameters on held-out seeds leaves active and fixed-$\theta$ accuracy **bitwise identical** at every value; the shielding margin is $0.0000$ throughout.
3. **Equalising one confounded parameter destroys the effect.** Equalising `theta_gate_init` alone collapses the reported shielding margin from $0.1333$ to $0.0000$. `test_noise_percolation_shielding` passes today because of the compound difference, not because of threshold adaptation.
4. **Where the mechanism is real, it is binary, not graded.** At the WP-5c calibrated optimum the margin is $0.0000$ at $\beta_\theta = 0$ and $0.2812$ ($\epsilon = 0.05$) / $0.3229$ ($\epsilon = 0.10$) for every $\beta_\theta \in [0.001, 0.20]$. Adaptation on-versus-off matters; the adaptation *rate* does not.
5. **The readout window is ruled out as the alternative explanation.** The margin holds at $0.25$–$0.35$ across `t_readout` $\in [6, 32]$; `t_readout` governs clean accuracy, not shielding.
6. **Scope.** The retrieval *capability* results of `EXP-2026-011a` are not in question. The noise-shielding *mechanism attribution* is.

- **Detection Mode**: Mechanical parameter search with a corrected objective, followed by targeted single-factor sweeps on held-out seeds.

## Primary Finding: The Ablation Varies Four Parameters

Verified directly against [src/experiments/exp_2026_011a_mva_associative_retrieval/config.rs](src/experiments/exp_2026_011a_mva_associative_retrieval/config.rs#L108-L131) (`match condition` blocks):

| Field | `ActiveAssociative` | `AblationFixedTheta` | Intended as part of the ablation? |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- |
| `beta_theta` | $0.05$ | $0.00$ | **Yes** — the declared ablation |
| `theta_floor` | $1.02$ | $0.50$ | No |
| `theta_gate_init` | $1.10$ | $1.00$ | No |
| `w_fwd` / `w_fb` | $1.10$ | $1.00$ | No |

The condition therefore lowers the gate's initial threshold and weakens forward and feedback coupling at the same time that it disables adaptation. Any accuracy gap between the two conditions is unattributable among these four factors.

## Supporting Evidence: The Named Mechanism Is Inert at the Published Operating Point

Sweep of $\beta_\theta$ at the shipped hand-tuned parameters (`beta_theta` $= 0.05$, `leak` $= 0.05$, `n_ref` $= 2$, `t_readout` $= 16$, `theta_gate_init` $= 1.10$, `w_inh` $= -1.50$), evaluated on held-out validation seeds $\lbrace 5000, \dots, 5007 \rbrace$:

| $\beta_\theta$ (12 values, $0.000 \to 0.200$) | Active acc. $\epsilon = 0.05$ | Fixed-$\theta$ acc. | Margin | Active acc. $\epsilon = 0.10$ | Fixed-$\theta$ acc. | Margin |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| every sampled value | $0.9792$ | $0.9792$ | $0.0000$ | $0.9010$ | $0.9010$ | $0.0000$ |

Not a digit moves across a $200\times$ range including $\beta_\theta = 0$. Active and fixed-$\theta$ produce **identical predictions on all 192 held-out sequences per noise level** (8 seeds $\times$ 24 sequences). Equalising `theta_gate_init` alone collapses the reported margin from $0.1333$ to $0.0000$.

Consequently `test_noise_percolation_shielding` ([tests/test_exp_2026_011a.rs](tests/test_exp_2026_011a.rs#L106)) passes on the compound between-condition difference, not on threshold adaptation.

Source: [data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/shielding_sweeps.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/shielding_sweeps.json) (`beta_theta_sweep_at_hand_tuned`).

## Secondary Finding: Where the Mechanism Is Real, It Is Binary Not Graded

The same sweep evaluated at the WP-5c calibrated optimum ([data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/params.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/params.json)):

| $\beta_\theta$ | Active acc. $\epsilon = 0.05$ | Fixed-$\theta$ acc. | Margin $\epsilon = 0.05$ | Margin $\epsilon = 0.10$ |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $0.000$ | $0.6979$ | $0.6979$ | $0.0000$ | $-0.0156$ |
| $0.001$ | $0.9792$ | $0.6979$ | $0.2812$ | $0.3229$ |
| $0.0025 \to 0.200$ (10 values) | $0.9792$ | $0.6979$ | $0.2812$ | $0.3229$ |

Adaptation on-versus-off matters decisively; the rate is irrelevant across the entire sampled range. **The transition is localised only to within $(0, 0.001]$** — no values below $0.001$ were sampled, so the transition shape inside that interval is unmeasured. Any claim resting on the *magnitude* of $\beta_\theta$ is unsupported.

Source: `beta_theta_sweep_at_wp5c` in [shielding_sweeps.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/shielding_sweeps.json).

## Ruled Out: The Readout Window Is Not the Alternative Explanation

Sweep of `t_readout` at the calibrated optimum:

| `t_readout` | Clean accuracy | Margin $\epsilon = 0.05$ | Margin $\epsilon = 0.10$ |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| $4$ | $0.4219$ | $-0.0052$ | $-0.0312$ |
| $6$ | $1.0000$ | $0.2812$ | $0.3229$ |
| $8$ | $1.0000$ | $0.2500$ | $0.2656$ |
| $16$ | $1.0000$ | $0.2760$ | $0.2708$ |
| $32$ | $1.0000$ | $0.3125$ | $0.3229$ |

The shielding margin is stable at $0.25$–$0.35$ across `t_readout` $\in [6, 32]$. At `t_readout` $= 4$ the circuit collapses to near chance (clean accuracy $0.4219$), taking the margin with it. `t_readout` therefore governs clean accuracy, not shielding, and does not substitute for threshold adaptation.

Source: `t_readout_sweep_at_wp5c` in [shielding_sweeps.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/shielding_sweeps.json).

## Provenance of the Discovery

The defect surfaced because a mechanical parameter search (WP-5a) was dispatched with an objective that encoded only the accuracy half of `test_noise_percolation_shielding` and omitted the ablation-ordering assertion. The optimizer drove `beta_theta` to $0.0010$ and still scored a held-out loss of $0.0000$ ([calibration_wp5a_de.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/calibration_wp5a_de.json), [params_wp5a.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/params_wp5a.json)), which prompted the investigation recorded here.

**The search did not create the defect; it exposed one that the original hand-tuning had masked.** The original session reached its published parameters through roughly 27 manual test-edit cycles over approximately 45 minutes ([docs/misc/audit_exp_010a_011a_execution_efficiency.md](docs/misc/audit_exp_010a_011a_execution_efficiency.md)), a regime in which a single-factor sweep of $\beta_\theta$ was never performed.

### Honest Negative Result on the Optimizer

Under the corrected WP-5c objective, the optimizer's advantage over random sampling is **not demonstrated**:

| Artifact | Optimizer | Evaluations | Calibration loss | Held-out validation loss |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| [calibration.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/calibration.json) | differential evolution | $24$ | $0.0443$ | $\mathbf{0.0443}$ |
| [calibration_full_budget.json](data/telemetry/EXP-2026-011a/calibration_full_budget.json) | differential evolution | $144$ | $0.0391$ | $0.0625$ |
| `calibration_random_search_full_budget.json` | random search | $144$ | $0.0417$ | $0.0625$ |

At a 144-evaluation budget the two methods are indistinguishable on held-out loss ($0.0625$ both), and both are **worse** held-out than the 24-evaluation result ($0.0443$) despite better calibration loss. The extra budget bought overfitting. No optimizer superiority should be claimed from this run.

## Required Resolution

Resolving the attribution requires re-running the noise-shielding contrast as a **genuine single-variable ablation**, in which the set of fields differing between the two conditions is a singleton:

$$\Delta(\text{active}, \text{ablation}) = \lbrace \beta_\theta \rbrace, \qquad \theta_{\text{floor}}, \ \theta_{\text{gate}}(0), \ W_{\text{fwd}}, \ W_{\text{fb}} \ \text{held equal across conditions.}$$

Concretely, in [config.rs](src/experiments/exp_2026_011a_mva_associative_retrieval/config.rs#L108-L131) the `theta_floor`, `theta_gate_init`, and `w_fwd`/`w_fb` `match condition` blocks must be collapsed to condition-independent constants for the `ActiveAssociative` / `AblationFixedTheta` pair, leaving `beta_theta` as the sole varied factor. The corrected contrast must then be re-evaluated on held-out seeds at both the hand-tuned and calibrated operating points, and `test_noise_percolation_shielding` re-derived against it.

This is a change to a pre-registered control definition and therefore requires a protocol amendment, not an execution-side edit.

## Uncertainty & Caveats

- **No confidence intervals were computed.** All sweep values in this report are point estimates over 8 held-out seeds $\times$ 24 sequences per cell (192 sequences per noise level). No bootstrap, no variance estimate, no significance test. The reported margins should be read as observed differences, not as estimated effects.
- **Two condition-specific differences remain uncontrolled even in the corrected comparison.** `theta_floor` and `w_fwd`/`w_fb` were outside the pre-registered search space (which covered `theta_gate_init`, `beta_theta`, `w_inh`, `leak`, `n_ref`, `t_readout` only), so their contribution was never measured and cannot be bounded from these artifacts.
- **The $(0, 0.001]$ transition interval is unsampled.** The binary-versus-graded characterisation holds only for the 12 sampled values; the behaviour below $\beta_\theta = 0.001$ is unknown.
- **Sweeps used 24 sequences per cell**, far below the 54,000-sequence factorial of the original run. They are adequate to demonstrate *identical* predictions and to localise a large margin shift, but not to resolve small effects.
- **Only the $\epsilon \in \lbrace 0.05, 0.10 \rbrace$ noise levels were swept.** Behaviour at $\epsilon = 0.01$ under a corrected ablation is unmeasured.
- **The WP-5c calibrated optimum is itself one point among several near-equivalent minima** and is not being proposed as a replacement operating point by this report.

---

## Recommendations

1. **Protocol amendment decision required (Theory & Orchestrator).** Whether the noise-shielding ablation should be redefined as single-variable is a pre-registration change and sits with the Sci: Theory & Protocol and Sci: Orchestrator roles. Execution should not amend a pre-registered control unilaterally.
2. **Do not re-tag `EXP-2026-011a` with calibrated parameters.** Re-running and re-tagging at the WP-5c optimum would publish a cleaner number over an uncorrected methodological defect. Re-tagging is inappropriate until the ablation is corrected. The previously planned re-run-and-re-tag work package is the **wrong action**, not merely a blocked one.
3. **Annotate, do not retract, `DIAG-2026-011a`.** Milestone 3.2 verification rests on Gates 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and the capability half of Gate 4, none of which are affected. Gate 4's attribution half and the fixed-$\theta$ control comparison should be flagged as pending re-derivation.
4. **Adopt objective-completeness as a protocol directive.** A pre-registered search objective must encode every acceptance assertion, including comparative and ordering constraints between conditions. An objective omitting an ablation ordering will be exploited by any competent optimizer, as WP-5a demonstrated.
5. **Audit earlier ablation conditions for the same defect.** Conditions defined via per-condition `match` blocks in prior experiment packages should be checked for unintended co-varying fields before their mechanism attributions are relied upon in downstream hypotheses.
