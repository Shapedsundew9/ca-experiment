## 4. Inner-Loop Parameter Discovery & Search Trajectory

- **Exploration Strategy**: Full factorial sweep across 7 conditions $\times$ 2 exposure durations $\times$ 30 deterministic pseudo-random seeds.
- **Observations during Exploration**:
  - Probing isolation was strictly enforced via substrate cloning (`substrate.clone()`), ensuring that the 10-trial stochastic perturbation measurements never perturbed somatic membrane potential or rolling rates of the active training substrate.
  - In unadapted baseline (`baseline_static`), minimum attractor separation remained low ($D_{\text{sep}} = 0.0493 < 0.05$), failing to separate distinct limit cycles cleanly.
  - In all plastic networks, continual learning significantly expanded attractor separation ($D_{\text{sep}} = 0.1045 - 0.1274$, $> +110\%$ improvement).
  - Under unconstrained joint training without metaplasticity (`control_joint_training_k0`), recurrent excitation saturated at $\theta_{\max} = 3.0$, producing runaway firing density ($\bar{\rho} = 0.2079 - 0.2290$).
  - With metaplastic consolidation enabled ($\kappa = 1.0, 5.0$), both sequential and joint training maintained perfect homeostatic equilibrium ($\bar{\rho} \in [0.05, 0.20]$ in 100% of runs).
