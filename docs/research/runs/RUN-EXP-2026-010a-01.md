## 4. Inner-Loop Parameter Discovery & Search Trajectory

- **Exploration Strategy**: Full factorial evaluation across 4 conditions $\times$ (2 streaming tasks $\times$ 4 lengths + 1 depth task) $\times$ 3 noise rates $\times$ 30 seeds = 3,240 runs ($201,600$ streamed evaluation sequences).
- **Observations during Exploration**:
  - `active_pda` maintained exact limit-cycle phase synchronization across all 30 seeds with zero pointer crosstalk ($\chi_{\text{ptr}} = 0.0000$) under clean baseline.
  - `baseline_finite_state` accurately parsed sequences of nesting depth $D \le 2$, but instantly overflowed and rejected deep sequences requiring depth $D \ge 3$, confirming that a finite-state controller with $D_{\text{cap}} = 2$ cannot recognize context-free Dyck languages.
  - `ablation_unshielded_stack` suffered immediate pointer mutual exclusivity breakdown ($\chi_{\text{ptr}} = 0.749$) due to bidirectional retrograde wavefront reflections, confirming the mathematical indispensability of refractory diode shielding ($N_{\text{ref}} = 2$).
  - `ablation_fixed_theta` exhibited catastrophic vulnerability to subthreshold background fluctuations ($\epsilon \in \lbrace 0.01, 0.05 \rbrace$), triggering spurious coincidence gate ignitions and collapsing classification accuracy to $50.4\%$.
